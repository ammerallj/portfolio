I’m a product designer based in Seattle, with a focus on interaction design. Over the last decade, I’ve designed products used by billions, shaping how people get things done and build community, while making social experiences more accessible.

Good interaction design lives in the seams: how products behave, the patterns worth sharing, and where context calls for a more bespoke approach.

Away from the screen, you’ll usually find me spending time with my velcro dachshund, Maeve, discovering new music, or wandering through art exhibitions. I’ve also found an unexpected appreciation for the quirks of running a small community since joining my condominium association’s board.
