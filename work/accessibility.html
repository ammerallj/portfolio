Shaping Accessibility Across Products

Duration
December 2023–July 2026
Role
Product Designer
Scope
Designed review workflows, product guidance, AI retrieval models, and Meta’s end-to-end public accessibility website.
Team
Engineering · Product · Research · Content Design · Marketing

Accessibility issues were often caught late, after teams had already made product decisions. As the stakes increased, having standards wasn’t enough. Teams needed to know what applied, when to act, and what to do next.

Approach

I focused on how accessibility guidance should change with the moment: when someone finds an issue, makes a product decision, asks for guidance, or looks for support.

What changed

  • Start the issue where it happens. I designed a lightweight capture flow that carried product context into the existing review workflow. The broader review and submission workflows saved over 1,700 hours of manual work.
  • Turn requirements into decisions. I translated accessibility requirements into practical guidance for the decisions teams were making in design and build.
  • Make AI-assisted guidance more reliable. I organized product questions around what people were trying to do, connected each one to authoritative guidance, and restructured the underlying content around that, eliminating deprecated retrieval results.
  • Start with the need, then the product. I launched Meta’s first centralized public accessibility destination, organizing 30+ features and 45 support articles around people’s needs first. View public accessibility work (opens in new tab) ↗
