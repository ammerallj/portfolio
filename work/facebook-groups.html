Helping People Navigate Communities

Duration
March 2022–April 2023
Role
Product Designer
Scope
Designed key Group Home behaviors for returning members, including top-of-home, contextual landing states, and Featured content.
Team
Product Design · Content Design · Product Management · Engineering · Research

Group Home had become crowded before members reached the content. Simplifying the landing experience meant preserving the cues people still needed to orient.

Approach

I focused on the returning member experience: what had to stay visible, what could collapse, and what should adapt based on how someone arrived.

  • Preserve the cues that help people orient. Keep the group identity visible enough for members to know where they are.
  • Let arrival context shape the landing state. Treat someone coming from a notification differently from someone opening the group directly.
  • Let freshness decide what deserves space. Keep important new content visible, then collapse it once it has been seen.

What changed

  • Members reached the group faster without losing orientation. I simplified the top of Group Home while preserving the identity cues members relied on to know where they were.
  • The landing experience could respond to how someone arrived. I explored different states for returning members so stronger content intent could bring the feed forward without treating every visit the same.
  • Featured content only kept its space when it was useful. I designed freshness-based behavior so new content stayed visible and seen content collapsed, increasing engagement.
